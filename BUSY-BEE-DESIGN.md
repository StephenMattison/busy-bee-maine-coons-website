# Busy Bee Maine Coons Design.md

## Brand Overview
**Domain:** busybeemainecoons.com  
**Brand Name:** Busy Bee Maine Coons  
**Tagline:** Busy Bee Maine Coons – Where Gentle Giants Find Their Forever Homes

**Logo Concept:** A friendly, stylized busy bee with Maine Coon features (tufted ears, bushy tail, large gentle eyes) combined with warm honey/gold accents.

---

## 1. Overall Design System & Visuals

### Color Palette (Warm, Luxurious, Nature-Inspired)
- Primary: Deep Forest Green (#1A3C34)  
- Secondary: Warm Honey Gold (#D4A017)  
- Accent: Rich Rust/Burnt Orange (#C44D2F)  
- Neutral Background: Soft Cream (#F8F4ED)  
- Text: Dark Charcoal (#2C2C2C)

### Typography
- Headings: Playfair Display (elegant serif)  
- Body: Inter (clean sans-serif)

### Overall Style
- Warm, trustworthy, premium yet approachable  
- Generous whitespace, large high-quality Maine Coon photography  
- Card-based layouts, subtle hover animations, mobile-first

---
