# Busy Bee Maine Coons SEO-Perfect Blog Post Template & Guidelines

## Purpose
Every blog post on busybeemainecoons.com must be engineered to:
- Rank on page one of Google for high-intent and informational keywords
- Establish unbreakable E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness)
- Drive qualified traffic that converts into kitten reservations and product purchases
- Support the overall business plan by building topical authority and funneling visitors to the Kittens, Products, and Breeder sections

## Required Blog Post Structure (Use This Exact Outline for Every Post)

### 1. Meta Title (55–60 characters max)
- Primary keyword near the front
- Example: "Maine Coon Kittens for Sale 2026: Ethical Breeders & Health Tested | Busy Bee Maine Coons"

### 2. Meta Description (150–160 characters max)
- Compelling hook + primary keyword + clear CTA
- Example: "Looking for Maine Coon kittens for sale from ethical, health-tested breeders? Discover our vetted partners, expert care guides, and premium giant-breed products at Busy Bee Maine Coons."

### 3. H1 Headline
- Must include primary keyword naturally
- Example: "Maine Coon Kittens for Sale in 2026: How to Find Ethical Breeders & Healthy Giants"

### 4. Introduction (150–200 words)
- Hook with emotional or surprising fact
- State the problem or desire (e.g., "Finding a healthy, well-socialized Maine Coon kitten can feel overwhelming...")
- Preview the value and include primary keyword
- End with a soft CTA link to current available kittens or breeder directory

### 5. Table of Contents (H2)
- Auto-generated or manual with anchor links
- 6–10 sections maximum for readability

### 6. Main Content Sections (H2 + H3)
Use this proven structure for most posts:

**H2: What Makes Maine Coon Cats So Special?** (or topic-specific)
**H2: [Primary Keyword Topic] – Complete Guide**
- Use bullet points, numbered lists, and short paragraphs (2–4 sentences max)
- Include data, statistics, and expert insights
- Add internal links to pillar pages (e.g., /kittens, /breeders, /products)

**H2: Health Testing Every Responsible Maine Coon Breeder Must Perform**
- Detailed, authoritative section with links to veterinary sources
- Builds massive E-E-A-T

**H2: [Subtopic] – Step-by-Step**
- Numbered steps with actionable advice

**H2: Common Mistakes When Buying Maine Coon Kittens**
- Builds trust and positions us as the expert guide

**H2: Recommended Products for Your New Maine Coon**
- Natural product placement with links to our store (high-margin oversized beds, grooming kits, etc.)
- Include images with optimized alt text

### 7. FAQ Section (H2)
- 6–10 questions with concise, helpful answers
- Use FAQ schema markup
- Target "People Also Ask" featured snippets

### 8. Conclusion & Strong CTA
- Summarize key takeaways
- Final powerful CTA:
  - "Ready to bring home your gentle giant? Browse our current Maine Coon kittens from ethical breeders at Busy Bee Maine Coons →"
  - "Shop premium Maine Coon gear designed for these magnificent cats →"
  - "Join the Busy Bee Insider Club for early access to new litters and expert advice →"

### 9. Author Box
- Professional bio highlighting real experience with Maine Coons
- Photo + credentials
- Social proof

## SEO & Content Rules (Non-Negotiable)

### Keyword Strategy
- **Primary Keyword**: Used in H1, first 100 words, meta title, meta description, and naturally 3–5 more times
- **Secondary Keywords & LSI terms**: "giant Maine Coon", "Maine Coon temperament", "Maine Coon health testing", "Maine Coon breeders near me", "Maine Coon care guide", "largest domestic cat breed"
- Target long-tail keywords with commercial intent

### Content Quality Standards
- Minimum 2,500–4,000 words for pillar posts (shorter 1,500–2,000 for supporting cluster posts)
- Every sentence must add value — no fluff
- Use conversational yet authoritative tone
- Include original data, personal experience, or cited studies
- Update older posts quarterly with fresh information

### Technical SEO Requirements
- Perfect heading hierarchy (never skip from H2 to H4)
- All images must have descriptive, keyword-rich alt text (e.g., "fluffy brown tabby Maine Coon kitten playing with oversized toy")
- Internal links to at least 5–8 relevant pages (kittens, products, specific breeders, other blog posts)
- External links to authoritative sources (veterinary sites, breed standards from CFA/TICA)
- Mobile-first formatting with short paragraphs and plenty of white space
- Target Core Web Vitals: Fast loading, especially hero images (use optimized WebP)

### E-E-A-T Signals (Must Be Obvious on Every Post)
- **Experience**: Share real stories, photos, or observations from Maine Coon owners/breeders
- **Expertise**: Cite specific health testing protocols, breed standards, and veterinary recommendations
- **Authoritativeness**: Reference official sources (CFA, TICA, veterinary journals)
- **Trustworthiness**: Transparent about ethical breeding, disclose any affiliate relationships, show real breeder partnerships

### Visual & Multimedia Rules
- Minimum 8–12 high-quality images per post (kittens, products, infographics)
- Use Grok Imagine for custom lifestyle and product shots when needed
- Include at least one comparison table or checklist
- Embed or link to short videos (kitten development, grooming tutorials)

### Conversion Optimization
- Place CTAs naturally after valuable sections (not just at the end)
- Use benefit-driven button text: "Reserve My Kitten Today", "Get My Free Maine Coon Care Guide", "Shop Giant-Breed Beds"
- Add trust badges near CTAs (health testing icons, "Ethically Bred", "Lifetime Breeder Support")
